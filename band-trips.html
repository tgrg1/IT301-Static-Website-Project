Background

With the Marching Royal Dukes, I have gone on two trips to travel and support the football team. One was a bowl game, which was the Boca Raton Bowl in 2024 (Wikipedia). Both of these trips were incredible experiences and I hope that we get to get more similar experiences within the next few years.
