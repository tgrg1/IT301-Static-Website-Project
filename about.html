You've found the about me page! My name is Tommy. I am currently a student at James Madison University in the IT program. I am also in the Marching Royal Dukes (JMU's marching band, a.k.a. Virginia's Finest).

My background in IT includes the classes I've taken here at JMU, including, as of the fall semester of my third year, IT301: Web Technologies, IT302: Societal and Ethical Issues in IT, and IT333: Advanced Networking for IT. These classes have helped me and will help me as I continue working in IT.

I have been to several places around the country and enjoy traveling and taking pictures. I created this website to document some of these places. I plan to visit many other places in the future!
